# HR Attrition Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition using Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into workforce distribution, employee demographics, and attrition patterns across different departments and genders.

The interactive dashboard helps HR professionals understand employee turnover trends and identify areas that may require further investigation.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition and retention.
- Monitor key HR performance indicators.
- Examine attrition patterns by gender and department.
- Understand workforce distribution across different job roles.
- Explore employee age distribution by gender.
- Present HR insights through an interactive dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data preparation and transformation.
- **Data Analysis** – Workforce and attrition analysis.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard presents four major KPIs:

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Employees | 1,470 |
| Active Employees | 1,233 |
| Attrition Count | 237 |
| Attrition Rate | 16.12% |

## Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

### 1. Employee Distribution by Department and Gender
A comparative analysis of employees across Research & Development, Sales, and Human Resources, segmented by gender.

### 2. Employee Distribution by Job Role
A visualization showing employee distribution across different job roles.

### 3. Employee Age Distribution by Gender
An analysis of employee age patterns, comparing male and female employees.

### 4. Attrition Analysis by Gender
A donut chart showing the distribution of employee attrition by gender.

### 5. Attrition Analysis by Department
A department-level breakdown of employee attrition.

## Key Findings

Based on the dashboard:

- The organization has **1,470 employees**, of whom **1,233 are active**.
- The overall employee attrition rate is **16.12%**.
- A total of **237 employees** have left the organization.
- Male employees account for 150 attrition cases (63.29%).
- Female employees account for 87 attrition cases (36.71%).
- Research & Development accounts for 133 attrition cases (56.12%).
- Sales accounts for 92 attrition cases (38.82%).
- Human Resources accounts for 12 attrition cases (5.06%).

## Business Recommendations

Based on the observed workforce and attrition patterns:

1. Investigate the factors contributing to employee turnover in Research & Development.
2. Examine employee retention challenges across different departments.
3. Conduct further analysis of gender-based attrition patterns.
4. Evaluate employee satisfaction, compensation, and career development opportunities.
5. Develop targeted employee retention strategies.

*Note: These recommendations identify areas for further investigation; the dashboard alone does not establish the causes of attrition.*
